SERVICE 02

Modular Kitchen Setup

Our modular stainless steel kitchen counters, prep tables, overhead shelving, and hot pass stations are engineered for rapid reconfiguration and ergonomic chef workflows. We eliminate hard-to-clean crevices and streamline movement between cold prep, hot lines, and dishwashing zones.

  • ✓ Laser-cut coved corners for effortless sanitation
  • ✓ Height-adjustable bullet feet and sound-dampened under-tops
  • ✓ Modular knock-down or fully welded custom configurations

SERVICE 03

Commercial Sink & Plumbing Engineering

Sanitation and washdown volume dictate the health compliance of any food establishment. Bakero fabricates heavy-gauge deep pot wash sinks, pre-rinse hand sinks, automated grease traps, and floor drain channels built to endure boiling oil, grease, and aggressive commercial cleaning agents.

  • ✓ Anti-splash high backsplashes with pre-drilled faucet holes
  • ✓ Integrated stainless scrap baskets and grease separators
  • ✓ Sloped drain boards and heavy-duty pre-rinse pull-down sprayers
Discuss Plumbing Specs

SERVICE 04

Custom Finishing & Architectural Fabrication

When standard dimensions fail to accommodate architectural columns, odd corners, or display show kitchens, Bakero's in-house fabrication team steps in. We provide custom satin scotch-brite finishes, copper PVD coatings, embossed trims, and radius bullnose edges tailored to your concept.

  • ✓ Bespoke bar counters, cocktail stations & buffet pods
  • ✓ Precision CNC laser cutting & seamless TIG welding
  • ✓ Custom aesthetic trims for open-concept live kitchens

SERVICE 05

Appliance Integration & Workstations

A cohesive kitchen functions like a single machine. We engineer consolidated service spines with centralized gas distribution manifolds, pre-wired circuit breaker conduits, and interlocking exhaust hood ventilation plenums that link your fryers, broilers, and ovens into an interconnected battery.

  • ✓ Centralized gas safety shutoff switches & fire suppression tie-ins
  • ✓ Concealed utility chases for electric, water, and drainage
  • ✓ Engineered exhaust air capture velocity calculations

Post-Install Support & Preventative AMC

Down equipment during peak meal service costs revenue and reputation. Bakero provides robust warranty protection, comprehensive Annual Maintenance Contracts (AMC), genuine OEM spare parts inventory, and rapid emergency dispatch to keep burners firing and chillers running.

  • ✓ Guaranteed response time within 4 hours for mission-critical faults
  • ✓ Scheduled quarterly thermal calibrations & safety audits
  • ✓ Comprehensive spare parts inventory stocked locally
